Confidence: **H** = the exact wording comes from the binding primary source. **M** = an official source, but secondary or not fully explicit. **L** = not officially confirmed.

## 11. Worked examples (calculated strictly from the rules)

Assumptions: application **11.11.2026–31.01.2027** (building cap WE 1 = 28,000 €, KGB 16 %, WP Grundförderung 30 %, Q1-2027 rules not yet active). Costs are eligible gross costs. Heat pump meets the TMA. zvE = average of 2023 and 2024.

**Algorithm:**
1. Building cap H = 28,000 + 15,000·min(n−1, 5) + 8,000·max(n−7+1, 0) for n ≥ 7.
2. Considered costs K = min(costs, H). For a measure covering only some of the units: H × affected units / n.
3. Basis = 30 % × K.
4. Self-used share A = K / n (WEG: min(MEA × K, K/n)).
5. Bonus rate b = KGB (16, if eligible) + EB (40/30/10 after the Familienzuschlag).
6. Cap per unit: if (30 + b) > cap (70 %, or 80 % if the effective zvE ≤ 30,000), then b = cap − 30.
7. Total = basis + b × A.
